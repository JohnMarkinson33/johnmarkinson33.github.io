finTB — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

finTB is a personal finance application developed and used exclusively by its owner (a private individual). It is not a commercial service and has no other users.

What data is processed

With the owner's explicit consent given through their bank's strong customer authentication, the application accesses the owner's own bank account information (account list, balances and transaction history) via the Enable Banking API, acting under PSD2 open banking rules.

How data is stored

  • All financial data is stored locally in the owner's browser (localStorage) on the owner's own devices.
  • Access credentials (API session) are stored in the owner's private Cloudflare Worker, accessible only to the owner.
  • No database of other persons' data exists — the application has a single user, who is also its operator.

What is NOT done with the data

  • No sharing with any third parties.
  • No advertising, profiling or analytics.
  • No processing of anyone's data other than the owner's own.

Consent and revocation

Bank access consent is time-limited and can be revoked at any time in the owner's internet banking or by disconnecting the session in the application.

Contact

The owner and operator of the application can be contacted via the e-mail address registered with the Enable Banking application.
